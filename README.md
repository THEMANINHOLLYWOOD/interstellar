# interstellar

one hour here is seven years at home. fly a lander down through the rain to a knee deep sea and climb out over a wave 1.2 km high

## what you can do

- fly the descent from orbit through the storm
- sit in the cockpit, take the controls, land it
- climb out over the wave when it comes
- diorama mode, see the whole thing from above

## run it

static, no build. `npx serve .` and open index.html
